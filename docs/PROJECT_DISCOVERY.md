### 3. Creative Direction: English Garden × Indian Wedding Editorial
- **Visual Harmony:** Blends the timeless refinement of bespoke British letterpress stationery (soft ivory, hand-pressed wax seals, botanical floral engravings) with the celebratory warmth of an Indian wedding (antique gold foil accents, deep sage foliage, warm earth tones, and celebratory presence).
- **Core Emotional Arc:**
  1. *Anticipation:* Receiving a wax-sealed bespoke envelope addressed to the guest.
  2. *Interactive Reveal:* Breaking/pressing the wax seal, opening the envelope flap in 3D, and drawing forth the gilded invitation card.
  3. *Pause & Intimacy:* Couple introduction with custom "I&A" monogram.
  4. *Family Warmth:* Symmetrical, dignified acknowledgement of both sets of parents.
  5. *Clarity & Significance:* High-impact editorial Save the Date section on rich deep sage.
  6. *Wayfinding:* Clean venue presentation with direct Google Maps integration.
  7. *Closure:* Heartfelt closing sentiment that leaves the guest feeling genuinely honored.

---

### 4. Layout & Mobile-First Principle
- The composition is strictly **mobile-composition-only** (390–430px content column, 320px minimum viewport).
- When opened on desktop screens (1366px, 1920px+), the invitation does **not** stretch into a wide dashboard. It remains centered as a luxury phone/stationery folio flanked by atmospheric deep sage and warm neutral textured margins.
