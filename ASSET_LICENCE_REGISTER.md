## 1. Compliance Statement & Legal Due Diligence

Every asset included in the project creative library has been inspected for commercial viability, intellectual property clarity, and redistribution legality. No unlicensed stock assets, watermarked previews, or rights-restricted materials are included in the final recommendation.

All 84 assets fall into four transparent, legally sound licensing categories:
1. **Public Domain / CC0-1.0:** Zero restrictions, commercial use permitted, no attribution required.
2. **SIL Open Font License (OFL-1.1):** 100% free for commercial digital web embedding, no royalties.
3. **MIT License:** Permissive open-source license allowing commercial use, modification, and distribution.
4. **Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY 4.0):** Commercial use permitted with general repository credit.

## 2. Licensing Breakdown by Source & Category

### 2.1 Typography (SIL Open Font License v1.1)
- **Asset Scope:** All 30 font files across Bodoni Moda, Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans, Playfair Display, Great Vibes, Alex Brush, and Pinyon Script.
- **Licence Terms:** OFL v1.1 grants permission to freely use, study, modify, and redistribute the fonts commercially. Web font embedding via `@font-face` is explicitly permitted without payment or license keys.
